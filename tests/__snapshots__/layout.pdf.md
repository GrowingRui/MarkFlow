# Field notes from the northern sites

The survey covered eleven sites along the river and took most of the spring. Water levels were lower than in any year since records began, which changed where the teams could camp. Most sites were reached on foot.

Two sites were skipped after a storm.

| --- | --- | --- |
| North bend | 24 | Done |
| Mill pond | 18 | Done |
